Where We Go

TOP
DESTINATIONS

Six iconic Albanian landscapes, each with its own soul and story. Kev knows them all like the back of his hand.

Must Visit
01

Albanian Riviera

Pristine turquoise waters and hidden beaches stretching along the Ionian Sea. Dhermi, Himara, Ksamil — Europe's last truly unspoiled coastline.

02

Berat

The City of a Thousand Windows. A UNESCO World Heritage site with Ottoman architecture frozen in time on a dramatic hillside.

03

Valbona Valley

The Albanian Alps at their most dramatic — a glacier-carved valley of emerald rivers and jagged peaks.

04

Gjirokastër

The Stone City. A living museum of Ottoman fortresses, cobblestone streets, and hilltop castles.

05

Lake Koman

A fjord-like lake cutting through sheer mountain walls. The ferry ride rivals the finest in Scandinavia.
